# Product Brief: RMI Health Dashboard (Living Document)

## Problem Statement
Health monitoring in the RMI is often fragmented. Decision-makers lack a unified, visual way to see performance scores and resource distribution across the islands in real-time.

## Vision
To provide a "single source of truth" for health facility performance in the Marshall Islands through an interactive, maps-first dashboard.

## Key Goals
- **Geospatial Visibility**: Map-based tracking of all facilities.
- **Historical Context**: Trend analysis for individual centers.
- **Resource Management**: Tracking of Health Assistants and personnel.
- **Accessibility**: Simple, responsive UI for various user groups.
